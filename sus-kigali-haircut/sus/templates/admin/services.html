How commission is worked out

Each service carries its own worker percentage, because the shop does not make the same margin on everything. For every sale the system looks at:

  1. the service's own percentage (set below) —
  2. otherwise the worker's own rate (Admin › Workers) —
  3. otherwise the shop default of {{ default_rate }}% (Admin › Settings).

A visit with several services pays each one at its own percentage and adds them up. Tips are always 100% to the worker.

Tip: deactivating a service hides it from the public website without deleting history.
