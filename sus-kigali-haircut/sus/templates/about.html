Our Story

Confident, disciplined, craft-focused — a Kigali original.

A heritage shop, not a trend.

SUS Kigali Haircut was built on a simple belief: every person who sits in our chair deserves precision, respect, and a sharp result. From the classic barbershop fade to protective braiding and immaculate nails, our team treats every service as a craft.

We are proud to serve all of Kigali — men, women, and children — with transparent prices, honest advice, and the same barber-shop discipline our grandfathers trusted.
